# Design exploration — three concepts (self-authored, not yet rendered)

These are design intents, not screenshot evidence. Web Designer Pro requires three rendered 390/1440 explorations and a critique before selecting the production visual direction. No approval is claimed here.

## A — The Uptaco Counter (recommended)
- Source: real Harlem counter tilework, architectural identity, food prep.
- Ground: lilac / lavender field with purposeful terracotta geometry.
- Type: original Uptaco sculptural wordmark + compatible geometric sans, NOT generic display font.
- Richness: real food cut-outs, tactile counter materials, a floating in-context pickup control cutting across a real photograph.
- Motion: Kinetrell's 440ms "taco travels to bag" with a short snap on the counter; no checkout dependence.
- Why recommended: immediately recognizable in store and online, pairs editorial craft with ordering.

## B — The Morning City
- Source: lively city magazine story.
- Ground: white daytime editorial field and photography.
- Type: oversized readable grotesque with precise short headings.
- Richness: Harlem neighborhood street photographs and faces supplied by the restaurant.
- Motion: restrained location transition and playful map pins; no auto-location permission.
- Why not default: less visually ownable than Uptaco's physical counter motif.

## C — The Illustrated Breakfast Club
- Source: Raffi mascot and expressive packaging.
- Ground: deep purple ink field, orange illustration signatures.
- Type: custom brand wordmark and bold high-contrast supporting labels.
- Richness: licensed Raffi artwork and flourishes, not code doodles replacing food.
- Motion: Rive-animated Raffi acknowledgement post-confirmation; loading still static accessible.
- Why not default: could become mascot-heavy and obscure actual food/orders.
