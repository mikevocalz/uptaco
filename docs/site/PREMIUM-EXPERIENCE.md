# Uptaco / Premium web experience — proposal v1

Status: design and engineering brief; NOT proof of a rendered, tested, or deployed website. This is a website-first product. The only acceptable brand is Uptaco's newer organic black/purple wordmark with lavender/orange and approved real food photography; the original red sun-taco logo is retired.

## Position: Uptaco's digital front counter
The site should feel like a remarkable neighborhood breakfast place and work like the best direct-ordering platforms. Harlem first without erasing Midtown; visual storytelling should never slow down checkout. Audience clusters:
- On-the-go Harlem customer: location, live availability, quick pickup
- Repeat Midtown/Harlem guest: one-tap reorder and saved configurations after opt-in
- Office organizer: group breakfast, catering lead-time compliance, payment/receipts
- Café operator: recurring refrigerated wholesale supply through Uptaco's current Odeko channel
- Staff: location-scoped production queue, handoff, courier exceptions and reconciliation

## Homepage copy architecture
1. Brand wordmark + photo of actual Uptaco taco on terracotta/lavender branded surface. Heading: "Breakfast tacos, made for New York mornings." Secondary text: "Hand-pressed tortillas, breakfast burritos, coffee, matcha, and more. Find us in Harlem and Midtown." Primary "Order Harlem pickup", secondary "Choose your location". Do not put fictional savings or opening hours here.
2. Real signature food story: 3 verified items from live menu when provider connected. "A morning worth getting up for." Must show actual food, never fake product renders.
3. Pickup versus delivery selector: clear outcomes, precise fulfillment rules, location selection first, route to merchant-verified checkout.
4. City presence: Harlem 300 W 135th St at St. Nicholas; Midtown 550 Madison Ave plaza. Store hours come only from merchant/verified dynamic data. No assumed open-now.
5. Catering: "Make the morning meeting the good part." Real catering packages and item-specific 36/48/72hr windows; HoneyCart order-through until direct module approved.
6. Café partners: "Good food, already handled." The Odeko B2B model and verified founder story; dedicated apply/contact path.
7. Human story: Derek and Dan, the Uptaco idea, hand-pressed tortilla process; approved identity assets.
8. Footer: locations, contact, ordering paths, menus, accessibility, privacy, allergen and account controls.

## No fake pricing or claims
HoneyCart offers real Classic, BEC, Plant Chorizo tacos, pastries, chilled coffee/matcha and breakfast boxes; the live Square catalog must provide consumer products, modifiers, prices and availability. Product descriptions must label allergens accurately and never promise cross-contact protection. Don't display Uber Eats marketplace prices as Uptaco direct prices.

## Distinctive digital design
Site style: the Uptaco counter; black sculptural typography on lavender, terracotta tile rhythm, photograph cut-outs and the mascot Raffi as a purposeful order-status character. Aerial food imagery with natural, tactile lighting, not synthetic AI tacos. Three depth planes: tile/purple editorial backing; actual food photography; foreground order selector intersecting the crop. Avoid black pills, dark+condensed+red slop, gratuitous 3D globes, endless marquees, generic app screenshots.

Art direction: editorial food photographs with 45-degree tabletop product and a crisp on-counter hero, photographed at the actual restaurant; unify natural daylight and warm white balance. Use genuine Uptaco approved logo and mascot vectors; original fonts licensed. Provisional colors are lavender #C68EDB, purple #624681, orange #EC5B27, terracotta #AA6545, pale lilac #F2E5F5, ink #17121B. Recalibrate after receiving official colors.

## Product differentiation
- "My Morning": last verified order and repeat with required modifiers/current prices revalidated; never silent purchase
- "Pick-up Pass": simple identity/pickup code, order status via provider, clear ready/collected not conflated with delivered
- Group breakfast: use eligible Square Online group ordering rather than another concurrent cart unless the provider permits an API integration
- Gift Uptaco: digital Square Gift Cards integration, balance and scheduled delivery after merchant scope approvals
- Rewards: if Square Loyalty is enabled, Square is the balance ledger, Payload keeps read models and campaign content, not independent accrual
- Office + café portals are separate from the consumer cart because quotes, lead times and settlement rules differ

## Business value
Priorities: verified pickup completion rate, repeat direct guests, catering requests/fulfillment, café partner leads, time-to-order, abandonment, modifier mistakes, Square POS order latency, courier exceptions. Do not invent baseline numbers or guaranteed lifts.
